## Payout terms, defined

| Term | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| RTP | Return to player. The percentage of all money wagered that a game returns over its lifetime. Theoretical, long-run, and never a prediction of your session. |
| House edge | 100% minus RTP. The operator's expected share of everything staked. |
| Volatility / variance | How returns are distributed. Low pays often and small; high pays rarely and large. |
| Hit frequency | How often a spin produces any win at all, typically 20–30%. Separate from RTP. |
| Max win | The cap on a single game round, expressed as a multiple of stake — 5,000× is common, 50,000× exists. |
| Turnover | Total money staked, not money lost. NZ$4,000 of turnover on a 96% game costs about NZ$160. |
| Game weighting | How much a game counts toward bonus wagering. Pokies usually 100%, table games often 10% or zero. |
| Payout percentage | Used loosely by casinos to mean a site-wide average. It is not audited or standardised — treat it as marketing, not data. |
