# How We Review Online Casinos: Our Testing Methodology

> ℹ️ **In one paragraph**We open a real account at every casino we rank, deposit our own money in New Zealand dollars, play, request a withdrawal, and record the timestamp at each stage. We read the full terms and conditions, verify the licence against the regulator's register, and test the responsible gambling tools. Sites are scored out of 5 across five weighted categories and re-tested quarterly. No operator can pay for a ranking position, and any site that stops paying players is removed the same week.

## The five scoring categories

| Category | Weight | What we test |
|---|---|---|
| Payouts & banking | 30% | Real-money deposit and withdrawal with timestamps at request, approval and receipt. NZD support, fees, minimums, method availability from a New Zealand address, and whether a pending period exists. |
| Bonus fairness | 25% | Wagering multiple and what it applies to, game weighting, max bet while wagering, expiry, max cashout, excluded methods, and any term that contradicts the advertised offer. |
| Games & software | 20% | Verified title count, studio spread, live dealer provider, published RTP, and whether the high-RTP build of a popular pokie is the one actually served. |
| Trust & safety | 15% | Licence checked against the regulator's own register, SSL, published operating company, complaint history, KYC behaviour, and whether RG tools are reachable within three clicks. |
| Experience | 10% | Mobile browser performance on a mid-range Android over a typical New Zealand connection, lobby search and filters, support response time in NZ hours, and friction from landing to first spin. |

## The withdrawal test, step by step

1. Register a new account with genuine details from a New Zealand address and IP.
2. Complete identity verification immediately, and record how long approval takes.
3. Deposit a standard test amount in NZD, and separately in crypto where supported.
4. Play a mixed session across pokies, a live table and one instant game.
5. Request a withdrawal and record three timestamps: request submitted, operator approval, funds spendable.
6. Repeat once more from the same account to measure a non-first withdrawal.
7. Where a bonus was claimed, attempt to clear it and record every obstacle encountered.

Median results across all sites are published on our [fast payout casinos](/fast-payout-casinos/) page. Where a figure could not be verified, we say "check current terms" rather than estimating — a made-up number is worse than an admitted gap.

## What gets a casino excluded or removed

- Failure to pay a verified winning withdrawal, or repeated unexplained delays.
- A licence that cannot be verified on the regulator's register, or one that lapses.
- Terms that contradict the advertised offer in a material way.
- Confiscating winnings on a technicality that was not clearly disclosed.
- Missing, broken or hidden responsible gambling tools.
- Targeting self-excluded players, or marketing that ignores an opt-out.

Removal is immediate and does not wait for the next quarterly review cycle.

## What our testing cannot tell you

Stating the limits is part of being useful. Four things our process does not establish:

- **Long-run solvency.** A site paying today can fail next year. We re-test quarterly, which narrows the window but does not close it.
- **How you specifically will be treated.** Our withdrawals are ordinary amounts from ordinary accounts. A five-figure win may meet review processes we never triggered.
- **Whether a game is fair.** RNG certification belongs to the studio and its testing lab, not to us or the casino. We check that certified studios are being used; we do not audit the games.
- **Anything about the future.** Bonus terms, ownership and payment methods change without notice. Every figure we publish carries a date for that reason.
