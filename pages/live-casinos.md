## How a live casino actually works

Worth understanding, because it explains both why live games are harder to rig and why they behave differently from pokies.

### The studio

A physical room — Evolution's are in Latvia, Malta, Georgia and Canada — with real tables, real dealers and multiple cameras. The dealer is an employee of the game provider, not of the casino you signed up with. Dozens of casinos stream the same table simultaneously, which is why you will see identical dealers across different brands.

### Optical character recognition

Cameras read the cards and the wheel in real time and convert them to data. That data drives the betting overlay on your screen and settles every bet automatically. It is also why results are auditable after the fact: every round has a video record and a data record that must agree.

### The betting window

Each round opens a fixed window — typically 12 to 20 seconds — during which all seated players place bets. When it closes, the dealer acts. You are not playing against an algorithm deciding your outcome; you are watching a physical event and betting before it happens.

### Bet behind and unlimited seats

Blackjack tables have seven seats. When they are full, "bet behind" lets you back another player's hand — you get their outcome without their decisions, which is fine if they play basic strategy and expensive if they do not. Infinite Blackjack removes seats altogether and gives everyone the same dealt hand.
