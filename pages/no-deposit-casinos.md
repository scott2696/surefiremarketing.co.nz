## The four kinds of no deposit offer

They are not interchangeable, and the differences decide what you can realistically walk away with.

### Free spins on a nominated pokie

The most common. A fixed number of spins — usually 20 to 50 — on one game the operator chooses, at a fixed stake of around NZ$0.10 to NZ$0.20. Winnings convert to bonus credit with wagering attached. ### Free bonus credit

A small cash-equivalent balance, typically NZ$10 to NZ$25, playable across a wider range of games. More flexible than spins, usually carrying higher wagering to compensate, and almost always excluded from live dealer and table games.

### No deposit free spins with no wagering

Rare and genuinely valuable. Winnings pay as withdrawable cash rather than bonus credit. Always small — ten spins is typical — and usually capped, but there is no turnover to grind. If you see one, it is worth more than a nominally larger wagered offer.

### Registration cashback or risk-free first session

Not strictly no-deposit, but adjacent: you fund an account and the operator refunds losses up to a limit if the first session goes badly. Read whether the refund arrives as cash or as bonus credit — that single word changes its value entirely.
